Sculptor: storyboard

Six moments in one commission, from an untouched block to the reveal. The stone drawings are placeholders for the real-time render; the HUD, panels and controls are the design commitment. Controls marked try it work.

1. The commission try it

Empty state. A fresh block sits on the banker under the window light, slowly turning. One prompt, a stone, a sculptor. Built in Sprint 3.

Commission accepted. The sculptor is sketching.

2. Sketching on the block

Before any stone comes off, the sculptor draws front and side profiles. They appear as charcoal on the block faces and pinned to the wall, and later looks can overlay them. Built in Sprint 3.

3. Roughing out try it

The main watching state. Corners come off in slabs that fall and break, then the point chisel works down to the margin. The subtitle is the sculptor's own thinking. Speed stretches or compresses the hand's work; dragging the canvas takes the camera until you let go. Built in Sprints 3 and 4.

4. Stepping back to look try it

When the sculptor asks to look, the hand stops, the camera pulls back and walks to the same viewpoints, and the notebook shows exactly what the model was sent: neutral clay renders with a centimetre grid and the outline of the form it is aiming for. Built in Sprints 2 and 3.

6. The reveal and the replay try it

The sculptor signs the base and names the piece. The floor is swept, the light sweeps once, and the whole commission becomes a scrubbable replay that can be exported as a film. A replay costs nothing to serve: no model calls. Built in Sprint 5.

Why this is not a Nuxt UI dashboard template: the screen is a full-bleed 3D canvas with a film-style HUD, the same shape as Voice World. The panels inside it stay on the rails: the notebook is UChatMessages with UChatReasoning and UChatTool rows inside a USlideover, the stage list is a vertical UStepper, transport uses UButton, USlider and USelectMenu, the commission card is a UCard with UInput, and toasts use useToast.
